*Last updated: February 15, 2026*

This Privacy Policy describes how leitzke.me ("the Site") collects, uses, and handles information when you visit [https://leitzke.me](https://leitzke.me).

## Information We Collect

### Analytics

The Site uses [Cloudflare Web Analytics](https://www.cloudflare.com/web-analytics/) to understand how visitors interact with the Site. Cloudflare Web Analytics is a privacy-first analytics service that does **not** use cookies or track individual visitors. It collects aggregate data such as:

* Page views and visits
* Referring website or source
* Browser and operating system
* Country-level geographic location
* Device type (desktop, mobile, tablet)

Cloudflare Web Analytics does not collect personally identifiable information. For more details, see [Cloudflare's Privacy Policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

### Comments

The Site uses [Giscus](https://giscus.app) for comments, which is powered by GitHub Discussions. If you choose to leave a comment, you will be directed to authenticate through GitHub. We do not collect or store your GitHub credentials. Your comment data is stored on GitHub and is subject to [GitHub's Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

### Local Storage

The Site stores your theme preference (dark or light mode) in your browser's local storage. This data never leaves your browser and is not transmitted to any server.

### Log Files

The Site's hosting provider may collect standard server log data, including IP addresses, browser type, referring pages, and timestamps. This is standard practice for web hosting and is used for security and performance monitoring.

## Cookies

The Site does **not** use cookies for analytics. Cookies may be used by:

* **Giscus** — session cookies when interacting with the comments system

You can control cookies through your browser settings.

## Third-Party Services

The Site uses the following third-party services:

| Service | Purpose | Privacy Policy |
|---------|---------|----------------|
| Cloudflare Web Analytics | Site analytics (cookie-free) | [Link](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/) |
| Giscus / GitHub | Comments | [Link](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) |

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes. The Site has no advertising.

## Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

* **Access** the personal data we hold about you
* **Request deletion** of your personal data
* **Object to processing** of your data

For GDPR (EU), CCPA (California), or similar requests, please contact us at the address below.

## Children's Privacy

The Site is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will remove it.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
